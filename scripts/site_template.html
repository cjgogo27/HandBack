Contact diagnostics have limits

These views were reconstructed from logged body poses, not captured at the contact peak. Their initial-frame registration was imperfect; they cannot prove precise visible overlap or hardware safety. Independent geometry checks found overlap evidence in four saved deep-contact states; one open link7 mesh could not support signed-volume certification.
